# Act II piece 3: The Orrery — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Act II's second arena: a solid terrace round a void with two counter-rotating rings over a captive sun whose flare sweeps the arena; wave 2 is a HOLD on a circle riding the inner ring; the run continues from the Nave into it and finishes there.

**Architecture:** Rings are ORBIT `Mover` segments (no new physics). A HOLD goal can follow a mover (`WaveGoal::mover`, `WaveDirector::goalPos()`). The flare is a new `ArenaShift::SOLAR` in `ArenaShifts` (timing, wedge test, wall cover) that also reuses SPEED_UP's ring speed-up; GameplayState applies its damage and draws it. The Orrery is built by `buildAct2` after the Nave, with a corridor between, a second basin and a void respawn.

**Tech Stack:** C++17 header-only game, SDL2, OpenGL 3.3/WebGL2, headless tests (`tests/test_game.cpp`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-act2-orrery-design.md`
