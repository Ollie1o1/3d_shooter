# Chapter 3 direction: superseded brainstorm

The early transit/Surveyor proposal has been superseded by the user's laboratory
chapter: ten rooms descending from a spacious hospital into dimensional crossings,
new flesh-like robots, a human-shaped final subject with its robot face, and a
choir-led high-speed boss fight opening the route into fractured dimensions.

[chapter-3-build-prompt.md](chapter-3-build-prompt.md).
