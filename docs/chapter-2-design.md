# Chapter 2: THE DEAD SPAN

Current direction: [Story revision — work left unfinished](story-overhaul.md).
The original Warden reuse has been replaced by Ballast, a separate road-clearance
sibling, model and fight. [Chapter 2 implementation notes](story-chapter-2.md)
cover the route, source files, editing commands and verification.

The chapter follows a road crew's unfinished work. A convoy stopped at the
subsidence; the relay's exit contact failed; turbine brakes seized; a request
for Ballast's replacement brake was returned because its model is unsupported.
Unit 24 follows the service route through those failures. Earlier units left
practical traces and records. The creator's identity and Unit 24's eventual
purpose remain open.

The route alternates a single convoy ambush, contact repair under bounded
pressure, a broken-span crossing, three shootable turbine locks, a quiet crew
checkpoint, Ballast's working cut and the far road. The freight shelter and
raised turbine maintenance deck give useful choices within the encounters.
Three repeated mixed-enemy waves are no longer the chapter's organizing pattern.

Ballast's silhouette and attacks come from its work: a broad grader blade,
articulated outriggers, cable weight and salvaged turbine. Clearance scars and
crew warnings establish it before the fight. Its dialogue starts as a worker's
warning, becomes a brake failure and ends with a small observation about the
road it had cleared. The environment carries the explanation.

Cosmetic ash, grit, tape, machinery and vent activity have a persisted detail
setting. Mandatory attack information and objective objects remain visible
at every setting. Ambient allocations leave a fixed reserve for combat.
